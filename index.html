About

I started building things on a screen before I understood what was running underneath. Games first, then the need to take them apart — and the discovery that the interesting part was always the layer below the surface.

I studied computer science, but most of what I use daily I taught myself: breaking builds, rebuilding them, pushing every tool until it stops being polite. The same instinct shaped the visual side — composition, type, brand systems.

Today those two halves are one practice. Every project I touch has to work under load and look deliberate at rest. What I care about is the seam between hardware, software and the people using them.
